# Privacy Policy – Sakuga Bot

**Last updated:** 13 November 2026
**Contact:** botsakuga@gmail.com

## Data I store
Sakuga Bot stores only:
- Your Discord **User ID**
- The **Server ID** where you played
- Your **quiz points**

This is used solely to maintain the server and global quiz leaderboards.
It is never sold or shared with other services.

## Messages
The bot reads messages to respond to commands and to check answers in the
animator quiz. Message content is not stored. Only the resulting points
(tied to your User ID and Server ID) are saved.

## Legal basis
Legitimate interest: running the leaderboard feature you take part in.

## Storage and retention
Data is stored unencrypted on [hosting provider], located in [country/region],
which acts as my hosting provider. Discord itself processes messages under
its own privacy policy. There are no backups.
Data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted [or: or [X] months of inactivity].

## Your rights
You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, or object
to its processing. Use the `!report` command (this sends your message and
User ID to me so I can verify the account) or email botsakuga@gmail.com.
I respond within one month. Reports are used only to handle your request
and are deleted afterwards. Deletion removes you from the server and global
leaderboards and is permanent. You can also complain to the Dutch Data
Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Servers
Server IDs are kept after the bot is removed so global leaderboard points
stay accurate. Server admins can ask for their server's data to be deleted.

## Age
You must be at least 13 (Discord's minimum age). I can't verify age; if you
are under 13, contact me and your data will be deleted.

## Changes
Updates are announced in the bot's status and the date above is updated.
