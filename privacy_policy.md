PRIVACY POLICY

I, or Sakuga Bot does not store ANY data except for:
Guild ID, User ID and the amount of points you have won with the quiz feature.
All this data is stored solely for the purpose of keeping track of your points on the public quiz points leaderboard
and will not be used for any other reason.

The data that is saved is saved for an indefinite amount of time and asking to delete this data can be done through the !report feature
(which proves that you are the user of the account you wish to delete from the database).

All data is stored on a third party website
